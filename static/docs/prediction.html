How the System Predicts

Ten Features That Describe a Swirl

The extractor reads the joint-torque time series and computes ten physical quantities, in three groups:

GroupFeaturesWhat they capture
Static liftmass_j2, mass_j3, lift_dyn_j3Gravity torque offsets from the lift; an inertial cue as the arm accelerates
Swirl dynamicsswirl_var_j2, swirl_var_j3, swirl_ptp_j3, phase_lag_s, rattle_eSlosh energy in the swirl; the swing of shifting mass; the lag between TCP motion and torque (viscous damping); rattle energy in the observable band
Grip and excitationgrip_comp, swirl_speedGripper compliance under load; the TCP swirl speed that drives the excitation

Three of the ten — the two static mass offsets and swirl speed — carry zero weight in the embedding. The static offsets sit below baseline noise, and swirl speed measures the macro's drive rather than the contents. The matcher works in the 7 informative dimensions; all ten stay in the vector for display.

The Taught Library

The library holds the taught signatures — 113 at last count, each a 10-D embedding with its label, material, category, and session tag, stored in MongoDB Atlas. Teaching writes a signature; the next prediction searches it. The offline evaluation used an 84/21 train/test split of the recordings.

Three Ways to Decide

Vector search over hand features. The query embedding goes to Atlas $vectorSearch, which returns the nearest taught signatures by cosine similarity. This path powers the vector-space plot and supplies the neighbor list on the decision card. On the held-out set it reaches 24% at the category level.

Shrinkage LDA on the same features. atlas.predict_lda standardizes the 10-D library, fits a shrinkage-LDA classifier in milliseconds, and predicts — refit on every call, so a teach takes effect immediately. This is the live decider. On the held-out set: 52% category, 43% material. The binary empty/nonempty number (86%) is a majority-class artifact — the classifier predicts nonempty for everything.

Learned embeddings, evaluated offline. Two unsupervised alternatives were baked off against the hand features: PCA-10 on raw torque-delta trajectories (43% material, matching hand features) and Chronos foundation-model embeddings (56% on swirl-only data, the best measured). Neither runs live — Chronos needs its own 2304-D index and torch on the venue machine.

What a Prediction Looks Like

A fresh recording comes off the swirl macro. The extractor computes the 10-D signature. predict_lda decides. The decision card shows the predicted category, the material, and the closest taught item — with the measured accuracy (52% category) rather than a per-prediction score, since the LDA probabilities are uncalibrated.
