Why it matters

Sealed containers are everywhere

Food, pharma, chemicals, parcels — containers whose contents matter and stay hidden under the lid. Haptic probing turns the arm itself into the sensor.

The hardware is already there

The channel already exists on every torque-reporting arm. The method is software: a fixed probing motion plus a feature map.

Any arm can play

The pipeline's only contract is a CSV schema — timestamped joint angles and torques with phase tags. Point your own macro at it (FEELS_MACRO_CMD), keep the extractor and vector memory, teach your own library.

Beyond the coffee cup

  • Parcel and package sorting: what's inside this box — liquid, fragile, or solid — before the gripper commits.
  • Assistive robotics: a robot handing you a container should know whether it's full, and of what.
  • Warehouse QC: flag a mislabeled or leaking container from its dynamics, inline, while it stays sealed.

The research angle

Most robot perception is vision-first. With a wrapped gift, humans shake it. This is that idea made concrete: active haptic perception — excite and listen — for when the thing you care about is hidden.
