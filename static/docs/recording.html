How to record

Use the tool: the dashboard's Mode 2 button runs the fixed macro (repeatable_grasp.py --object <label> --record --swirl-smooth). Nothing else to do by hand.

What to record

First, the confound set — four items at the same mass, different contents:

  • solid_150g — a rigid 150 g weight
  • water_150g — 150 g of water
  • candy_150g — 150 g of loose candy
  • soap_150g — 150 g of dish soap

These four weigh the same — the baseline reads identical. Separation comes from dynamics alone; that is the claim being tested.

Acceptance criteria

Good = the SWIRL phase is present and the baseline flags are clean. The tool refuses the rest.
