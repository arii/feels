How to record

Use the tool: the dashboard's Mode 2 button runs the fixed macro (repeatable_grasp.py --object <label> --record --swirl-smooth). Nothing else to do by hand.

What to record

Teach the items the demo needs: solid_150g, water_150g, candy_150g, soap_150g — the labels are names, not measured weights. Then the basics: empty_cup, water_half, solid_020g, screws_100g. Record 2–3 repetitions per item; repeats let the matcher average out run-to-run noise.

Acceptance criteria

Good = the SWIRL phase is present and the baseline flags are clean. The tool refuses the rest.
