The hackathon

Viam + MongoDB "Physical AI" robot hackathon, October 29–30 2026, at the Robotics Center of Silicon Valley in San Francisco. Two days, one xArm x850 (unit sfhack12), judging on functionality, ambition, creativity, modularity — and 5% Halloween.

Built in under two days

Day one: the team pivoted to the lidded-cup idea, wrote the deterministic swirl macro, and recorded the first experiment matrix — pick, swirl, place, repeated across water, weights, screws, and empty cups. By evening the pipeline ran end to end: torque telemetry → 10-D signature → Atlas vector search → prediction.

Day two: the dashboard, the docs site, the simulated-replay backup mode, and the evaluation bake-offs (hand features vs PCA vs Chronos, kNN vs LDA). Everything here — the feature extractor, the vector memory, the docs you are reading — was written, tested, and demoed inside 48 hours.
