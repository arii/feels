Approach

Deterministic Swirl Motion

The robot executes a single, repeatable sequence (pick up → swirl → place) to ensure every dynamic recording is strictly comparable.

10-Dimensional Physical Signature

The pipeline translates the raw torque time series directly into a 10-D vector, mapping every extracted feature to a distinct, observable physical property.

GroupFeaturesWhat they capture
Static liftmass_j2, mass_j3*, lift_dyn_j3Gravity torque offsets = mass estimate; inertial cue from the lift
Swirl dynamicsswirl_var_j2, swirl_var_j3, swirl_ptp_j3, phase_lag_s, rattle_eSlosh energy; shifting-mass swing; TCP–torque phase lag = viscous damping; rattle energy (observable band only)
Grip & excitationgrip_comp, swirl_speed*Gripper compliance under load; TCP swirl speed for excitation normalization

* Zero-weighted in the embedding — the static offsets sit below baseline noise, and swirl speed measures the fixed macro's drive rather than the contents. The matcher works in 7 dimensions.

Direct Torque-to-Category Mapping

The architecture maps raw joint torques directly to material categories.

Feature Extraction

The signature captures specific physical properties, including static lift mass estimates, slosh energy, viscous damping (phase lag), and gripper compliance under load.

Scaled Vector Retrieval

The system scales and L2-normalizes these features before executing a vector search. By zero-weighting static offsets, the matcher evaluates active dynamics across 7 highly informative dimensions.

Instantaneous Teaching

The system learns new items immediately by storing a single embedding in the library. The next query searches the updated vector space immediately.

Teach the items the demo needs via the dashboard's Mode 2 dropdowns (Name + Amount → composed label). Record 2–3 repetitions per item; repeats let the matcher average out run-to-run noise. Acceptance: the SWIRL phase is present and the baseline flags are clean — the tool refuses the rest.

Dynamic Refinement

A shrinkage-LDA classifier refits the library at every prediction, reporting the measured accuracy across three levels: general category, specific material, and closest taught item.
